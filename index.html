About Us

Your Home Away From Home in Addis Ababa

Hera Addis Hotel is a luxurious and elegant accommodation located in the heart of Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Situated in the Lancha area of Kirkos, we offer a serene retreat with stunning city views.

Since 2017, we've been providing exceptional hospitality with modern amenities, spacious rooms with wide windows overlooking the city, and personalized service that makes every stay memorable.

  • Prime location in Lancha, Kirkos
  • 15 minutes from Bole International Airport
  • Award-winning staff (9.3 rating)
  • Spacious rooms with city views
  • Complimentary breakfast & parking
